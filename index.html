about

About

Full-stack engineer in Tanta, Egypt, with 7+ years building single-page apps, mobile apps and RESTful APIs.

I grew up around computers and wrote my first code in preparatory school, in Visual Basic .NET. These days I care most about the part people touch, and I'm growing toward design engineering. See experience for where, and the principles below for how.

how I work

  1. Simplicity in complexity

    Make complex systems feel simple, intuitive, and effortless to use without losing depth.

  2. Attention to detail

    Treat small details as meaningful decisions. They often define the difference between good and great products.

  3. Interface craftsmanship

    Build pixel-perfect interfaces where spacing, structure, and interaction feel intentional and consistent.

  4. Engineering quality

    Balance speed with maintainability. Ship fast without creating fragile systems.

  5. Writing & knowledge sharing

    Share engineering and product thinking through writing, to transfer knowledge and reflect on real-world experience.

  6. Mentorship & growth

    Help junior and mid-level engineers grow through guidance, feedback, and practical engineering insight.

mentorship

I mentor junior and mid-level engineers and interns on React, React Native, Next.js and design patterns, and I've trained teams on AI-assisted workflows with Cursor.
